=== Creating a Modular "Core" Library ===
Instead of having your APIFeatures (Day 48) or AppError (Day 31) living inside one project, you are going to package them.
This allows you to update your logic in one place and have it reflect across all your future microservices.

=== Internal Shared Libraries ===
Professional teams at companies like Google or Meta use "Monorepos" or "Private Registries" to share code.
1. Separation of Concerns: Your business logic stays in the app; your "Infrastructure" logic stays in the library.
2. Semantic Versioning (SemVer): Learning how to version your code (e.g., v1.0.1) so that updates don't break old projects.
3. NPM Link: A tool to test your package locally in your app without actually publishing it to the internet yet.
