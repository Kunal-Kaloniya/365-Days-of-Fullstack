=== Helm & Templating ===
If Kubernetes is the OS, Helm is the "App Store" or npm.

=== Don't Repeat Yourself (DRY) ===
1. Charts: A collection of files that describe a related set of Kubernetes resources.

2. Values.yaml: Instead of hardcoding values in your YAMLs, you use placeholders like {{ .Values.replicaCount }}. You keep one set of templates and just swap the values.yaml for Dev, Staging, and Production.

3. Release Management: Helm tracks your installations. If a new deployment fails, you can run helm rollback to instantly return to the previous stable state of your entire stack.
