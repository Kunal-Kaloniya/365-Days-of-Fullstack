=== The Architectural Great Debate ===
When you start a company, you usually build a Monolith (one repo, one API, one database).
As you grow, that monolith becomes a "Big Ball of Mud." Today, we analyze when and how to break it apart.

=== Decomposition & Distributed Systems ===
1. The Monolith: Everything is in one place. Easy to deploy, easy to test, but hard to scale parts of it independently.

2. Microservices: The app is split by "Business Domains" (e.g., Auth Service, Payment Service, Inventory Service).

3. The Trade-off: Microservices solve "Scaling Problems" but introduce "Communication Problems" (Network latency, Data consistency, and Distributed Tracing).


MICROLAB
Design the communication flow for a "Microservices" Order system.
When a user buys something, how does the Order Service tell the Email Service to send a receipt without blocking the user?

The Solution: Event-Driven Architecture (Pub/Sub)
Instead of an HTTP call, the Order Service "publishes" a message to a Message Broker (like RabbitMQ or Redis Streams).
The Email Service "subscribes" to that topic and picks it up when it's ready.
