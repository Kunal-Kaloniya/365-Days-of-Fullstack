=== API Gateway vs. Service Mesh ===
As your app grows from 5 to 50 microservices, managing "North-South" (External to Internal)
and "East-West" (Service to Service) traffic becomes a nightmare.

=== Traffic Management ===
1. API Gateway (North-South): The "Front Door". It sits between the user and your services.
    (a) Responsibilities: Authentication, Rate Limiting, SSL  Termination, and Request Routing.
    (b) Tools: Kong, AWS API Gateway, Nginx.

2. Service Mesh (East-West): The "Internal Nervous System". It manages communication between your services.
    (a) The Sidecar Proxy: Every service gets a small "helper" (like Envoy) that handles the network.
    (b) Responsibilities: Service Discovery, Retries/Timeouts, Circuit Breaking, and Mutual TLS (mTLS) for internal security.
    (c) tools: Istio, Linkerd.
