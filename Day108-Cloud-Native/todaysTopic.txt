=== Kubernetes Ingress ===
If a Service is a phone extension, Ingress is the automated receptionist
that routes you to "Billing" or "Support" based on what you ask for.

=== L7 Routing ===
1. The Controller: Ingress requires a "Controller" (usually Nginx or Traefik) to actually move the traffic.

2. Path-Based Routing: myapp.com/api goes to the Backend Service, while myapp.com/ goes to the Frontend Service.

3. Host-Based Routing: api.myapp.com goes to one service, and dashboard.myapp.com goes to another.

4. SSL Termination: Ingress handles your HTTPS certificates in one central place, so your individual microservices don't have to deal with SSL.
